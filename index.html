The farm

A farm-direct feed source, a phone call away.

When the animals need feeding, you shouldn't have to drive an hour to a farm store.

Schafer Farm is a family operation in Ohio's 740 country selling animal feed straight off the farm. There's no warehouse and no call center. You talk to the people who handle the feed.

Supply changes with the season, so the quickest way to find out what's ready is to call or text (740) 509-3615. You'll get a straight answer on what's here, how much there is and the price.

Questions

Good to know

What do you have right now?

It changes through the year, so please call or text (740) 509-3615 and we'll tell you exactly what's on hand and the current price.

Do I need to call before coming out?

Yes, please. This is a working farm, not a storefront with posted hours. Call or text first and we'll set a time to meet you.

Can I order by text?

Yes. Use the text-an-order form above, or just text (740) 509-3615 with what you're feeding and how much you need.

Do you deliver or handle large orders?

Ask us. Tell us the amount and where you are, and we'll tell you honestly what we can do.

Where are you?

We're in southeast Ohio (the 740 area). We'll give you directions to the farm when we confirm your order.
